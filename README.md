# Top Video Analysis (Sports)


**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Sports Video Analysis, Tactical Analysis, Coaching, Performance Analysis & Athlete Development*  
**Last updated: August 2026**


This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Sports Video Analysis**. These tools help coaches, analysts, teams, and athletes capture, tag, annotate, compare, measure, and analyze sports video to improve tactical understanding, technical execution, player development, and performance.


**Examples** include Hudl, Dartfish, Nacsport, Coach Paint, Spiideo, Catapult Vision, LongoMatch, Kinovea, Hudl Sportscode, and Performa Sports.


**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source sports-video analysis applications, computer-vision projects, video annotation tools, motion-analysis software, tracking frameworks, and sports analytics libraries. Kinovea is a particularly strong open-source option for capture, annotation, comparison, tracking, and measurement, while LongoMatch is specifically oriented toward tagging and analyzing sports plays. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms


- **[Hudl](https://www.hudl.com/)**  
  Comprehensive sports video and performance-analysis platform for recording, reviewing, tagging, sharing, scouting, and analyzing games and training sessions.


- **[Dartfish](https://www.dartfish.com/)**  
  Sports video-analysis platform focused on video capture, tagging, telestration, biomechanics, comparison, and coach-athlete feedback.


- **[Nacsport](https://www.nacsport.com/)**  
  Professional sports-analysis software for tagging match events, creating custom analysis workflows, generating reports, and building video presentations.


- **[Coach Paint](https://www.coachpaint.com/)**  
  Sports coaching video-analysis tool specializing in telestration, drawing, tactical explanations, and visual feedback for athletes.


- **[Spiideo](https://www.spiideo.com/)**  
  Automated sports video platform combining AI-powered capture, recording, live streaming, analysis, and cloud-based video access.


- **[Catapult Vision](https://www.catapult.com/)**  
  Sports video-analysis and performance platform integrated with Catapult's athlete-performance ecosystem for combining video and performance data.


- **[LongoMatch](https://www.longomatch.com/)**  
  Sports video-analysis platform focused on tagging plays, reviewing events, creating playlists, and producing analysis presentations.


- **[Kinovea](https://www.kinovea.org/)**  
  Sports video-analysis application for capture, slow motion, comparison, annotation, tracking, and measurement. Kinovea is also available as open source. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}


- **[Hudl Sportscode](https://www.hudl.com/products/sportscode)**  
  Professional performance-analysis environment for live and post-match coding, video tagging, tactical analysis, and collaborative coaching workflows.


- **[Performa Sports](https://www.performa-sports.com/)**  
  Sports performance-analysis platform providing video coding, tactical analysis, reporting, and coach-focused performance insights.


- **[Coach Logic](https://www.coach-logic.com/)**  
  Collaborative sports-analysis platform for video sharing, feedback, coaching communication, and player development.


- **[Veo](https://www.veo.co/)**  
  AI-assisted automated sports camera and analysis platform designed to record matches and generate searchable game footage and performance insights.


- **[Trace](https://www.traceup.com/)**  
  Automated sports-video platform using cameras and AI to capture games, generate highlights, and provide player-focused analysis.
